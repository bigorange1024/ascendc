### 定稿标题 / 摘要（中文）

**标题。** 让Agent不再发散探索——预研项目中基于已验证能力快速高效构建高级实现

**摘要。** 将复杂研究交由Agent执行，相当于在开放符号空间中做无约束构造，当模型能力不够强大、或者任务比较复杂时，容易拉长工作周期、抬高推理开销。我们尝试在昇腾NPU上实现抗量子计算密码算法这种有多层依赖的算子链，由Agent承担实现方案探索与编码。实践中的问题是Agent在研究过程经常越界——引用尚未成立的前提、跨越依赖层次构造对象、把局部对拍当作整体完成等等，没有完全基于从已证明前提进行合法派生，且派生系统往往不满足正确性。我们基于形式语言与自动机构造了一种解决方案，把完全依靠Agent直觉和模型能力完成工程的过程，转变为一套基于数学的、可重用的、可对照实验的方法论，使Agent在复杂预研任务中，只沿已验证能力及其合法组合生长实现，从机制上压缩和消解幻觉空间。实验结果显示，Agent基于该方法论实现的算子既能保持代码正确性，又能显著提升开发效率、降低token消耗、并且不高度依赖模型能力。实验效果表明，把开放式编码限制为有向图上的可判定派生，是提升AI在预研开发类工作中工作效率、降低消耗的一种有效途径。

### English

**Title.** Stopping Agents from Open-Ended Exploration: Building Higher-Level Implementations Efficiently from Verified Capabilities in Pre-Research

**Abstract.** Delegating complex research to an agent amounts to unconstrained construction in an open symbol space. When the model is insufficiently capable, or the task is relatively complex, this readily lengthens the project cycle and raises inference cost. We consider implementing post-quantum cryptographic algorithms on Ascend NPUs—operator chains with multi-layer dependencies—where the agent explores implementation strategies and writes code. In practice the agent frequently oversteps during the research process: citing premises that have not been established, constructing objects across dependency levels, treating local cross-checks as global completion, and so on. The work is not fully derived from proven premises, and the derived system often fails to satisfy correctness. Drawing on formal languages and automata, we turn an engineering process that relies solely on agent intuition and model capability into a mathematical, reusable, and experimentally comparable methodology, so that in complex pre-research tasks the agent grows implementations only along verified capabilities and their admissible combinations, thereby compressing and dissolving the hallucination space at the mechanism level. Experiments show that operators implemented under this methodology preserve code correctness, while substantially improving development efficiency, reducing token consumption, and remaining not highly dependent on model capability. The results indicate that restricting open-ended coding to decidable derivation on a directed graph is an effective way to raise productivity and lower cost when AI is used for pre-research development.
